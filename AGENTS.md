# Gemma Hackathon – Cloud Next Extended
## Participant Guide

Welcome to the Gemma Hackathon at Cloud Next Extended. In this 3-hour challenge, you and your teammate will build a working prototype that uses Gemma in a meaningful and demonstrable way.

The goal is to create something useful, creative, and easy to present.

---

## Event Overview

- Team size: 2 participants
- Total build time: 3 hours
- Submission time: 30 minutes
- Showcase time: 30 minutes
- Format: Build a working demo using Gemma and Antigravity
- Goal: Deliver a clear, practical prototype with strong user value

## What You Can Build

You may build any project idea as long as Gemma plays a central role in the solution. A focused and reliable prototype is better than a broad but incomplete one.

## Project Requirements

Your project must:
- Use Gemma meaningfully in the main user experience
- Be demonstrable or verifiable
- Use Google Cloud products only where they add clear value
- Include a public repository or Kaggle notebook
- Be supported by a public demo video and blog

## Submission Requirements

Each team must submit:
1. Kaggle Project Write-up
2. Labtracer submission

The submission should include:
- Project name
- Team members
- Problem statement
- Proposed solution
- How Gemma is used
- Antigravity workflow and development impact
- Architecture overview
- Demo link and public blog link

## Judging Criteria

Projects will be judged based on:
- Gemma integration
- Innovation and impact
- Functionality and usability
- Agentic developer workflow
- Google Cloud architecture
- Clarity of communication and submission quality

## Prizes

- 1st place: US$900 per team
- 2nd place: US$600 per team
- 3rd place: US$500 per team
- Top 10 teams: Google swag

## Important Notes

- Do not share private credentials or workspace details
- Test all public links before submission
- Make sure your demo video, repository, and blog are public and accessible
- Record your demo before event-day access expires

## Closing

Stay focused, build a strong demo, and make sure Gemma is clearly visible in the experience. A simple, reliable prototype with a compelling story will stand out more than an overly ambitious idea.
